Made to work for every body

Z Orbit is a free, independent movement and reflection tool — it's not medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a replacement for a doctor, physical therapist, or other qualified professional.

There's no such thing as "not enough range of motion" here. Every move below can be done seated, lying down, one side at a time, in the smallest range that's comfortable, or just imagined and breathed through if that's what today calls for. If you're managing limited mobility, recovering from something, or spending most of your day in bed, this still works — you're not required to match a "full" version of anything. Small and real beats big and forced, every time.

Pick whatever variation fits you right now, keep clear space if you're moving, and stop if anything feels painful, dizzying, or unsafe.

The idea behind this

Z Orbit treats practice as something you do for yourself, on your own terms — not a test, not a competition. The goal is steadiness and a few more good choices in how you move through your day, not proving anything to anyone.

Doing less on a hard day isn't giving up — it's just accurate. Control beats force, and rest is part of the practice, not a break from it.

Why "range" isn't the point

Finished sessions add to your own private time total. That total isn't a ranking or proof of anything — it's just a record that you showed up, in whatever way worked that day.

The "how much do you want packed in" setting changes how many moves get pulled into a session — it's a game setting, not a measurement of your body or your effort.
